隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

本政策说明 DCM 文件查看器如何处理您主动导入的文件及元数据。This policy explains how DCM File Viewer handles files and metadata that you choose to import.

简体中文

本隐私政策适用于由 tingting chen（以下简称“我们”）提供的“DCM 文件查看器”iOS 与 iPadOS 应用。

核心原则 您导入的 DICOM 像素数据及患者、检查、序列和图像元数据仅在设备上处理。应用不会把这些内容上传给我们或第三方。

1. 应用处理的内容

只有在您通过系统“文件”选择器主动选择文件或文件夹后，应用才会读取其中的 DICOM 内容。DICOM 文件可能包含影像像素、姓名、患者编号、检查日期、机构名称、检查描述以及其他敏感或身份信息。

2. 我们不收集的数据

我们不会通过本应用收集、接收或保存您的 DICOM 文件、导入影像、敏感信息、身份信息、设备标识符或使用行为。应用不包含账号系统、广告、分析、归因或跟踪工具，也不会把使用数据出售或共享给数据经纪商。

3. 本地处理与存储

导入内容会复制到应用的私有容器并使用随机文件名保存。应用请求系统的数据保护能力，并将本地影像资料排除于 iCloud 备份。内容会一直保留到您在应用内删除相应序列、清空全部资料或卸载应用。应用进入后台时，默认显示隐私遮罩；您也可以在资料库中隐藏患者姓名。这些设置不会修改原始 DICOM 文件。

4. “文件”提供方

您可以从“在我的 iPhone/iPad 上”、iCloud Drive 或其他已安装的文件提供方选择内容。如果您选择云端文件，相关提供方可能需要先把文件下载到设备。该过程由您选择的提供方依照其自身条款和隐私政策处理，并不代表本应用把影像上传给我们。

5. 网络、第三方服务与备份

当前版本不使用我们的服务器，不集成第三方分析或广告 SDK，也不提供云同步。应用导入的资料被标记为不进入 iCloud 备份。Apple 和您选择的文件提供方可能按照各自政策处理与其系统服务有关的技术信息。

6. 您的控制

  • 在资料库中删除单个影像序列。
  • 在“设置”中删除全部已导入的影像和本地索引。
  • 关闭患者姓名显示或保留后台隐私遮罩。
  • 卸载应用，以移除应用容器内保存的内容。

由于我们不接收这些本地内容，我们无法代替您查看、导出或删除设备上的文件。

7. 安全

应用使用 iOS/iPadOS 提供的应用沙盒和数据保护能力来降低未经授权访问的风险。但任何设备或存储方式都无法保证绝对安全。请为设备设置强密码，保持系统更新，并避免在普通电子邮件或不受控渠道中发送可识别患者身份的文件。

8. 用途限制

本应用是一般文件查看与学习参考工具，不用于诊断、治疗、用药或其他专业决策。如需对影像进行诊断性解读，请咨询具备相应资质的专业人员。

9. 政策变更

如果应用的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策和页面顶部的日期，并在适用时更新 App Store 的隐私披露。新增云服务、账号、分析或支持文件上传前，将重新评估并披露相关处理。

English

This Privacy Policy applies to the DCM File Viewer app for iOS and iPadOS provided by tingting chen ("we," "us," or "our").

Core principle DICOM pixel data and patient, study, series, and image metadata that you import are processed only on your device. The app does not upload this content to us or third parties.

1. Content processed by the app

The app reads DICOM content only after you explicitly select files or folders with the system Files picker. DICOM files may contain image pixels, names, patient identifiers, study dates, organization names, study descriptions, and other sensitive or identifying information.

2. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, receive, or retain your DICOM files, imported images, sensitive information, identifying information, device identifiers, or usage activity through the app. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics, attribution, or tracking tools. We do not sell or share app usage data with data brokers.

3. On-device processing and storage

Imported content is copied into the app's private container and stored under randomized filenames. The app requests operating-system data protection and excludes its local image library from iCloud Backup. Content remains until you delete a series, erase all imported data in the app, or uninstall the app. A privacy shield covers the app when it enters the background by default, and you can hide patient names in the library. These settings do not alter the original DICOM files.

4. Files providers

You may select content from On My iPhone/iPad, iCloud Drive, or another installed Files provider. If you select a cloud file, that provider may first download it to your device. Your chosen provider handles that process under its own terms and privacy policy; it does not mean this app uploads the images to us.

5. Network, third-party services, and backup

The current version does not use our servers, integrate third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, or provide cloud sync. Imported app data is marked for exclusion from iCloud Backup. Apple and your chosen Files providers may process technical information connected with their system services under their own policies.

6. Your controls

  • Delete an individual image series from the library.
  • Delete all imported images and the local index in Settings.
  • Hide patient names or keep the background privacy shield enabled.
  • Uninstall the app to remove content stored in its app container.

Because we do not receive this local content, we cannot view, export, or delete files on your device for you.

7. Security

The app uses the iOS/iPadOS app sandbox and data-protection capabilities to reduce unauthorized access. No device or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device with a strong passcode, keep the operating system current, and do not send identifiable patient files through ordinary email or uncontrolled channels.

8. Use limitations

This app is a general file-viewing and educational reference tool. It is not intended for diagnosis or treatment and must not be used for medication or other professional decisions. For diagnostic interpretation of an image, consult a qualified professional.

9. Policy changes

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and the date above and, where applicable, the App Store privacy disclosure. We will reassess and disclose relevant processing before adding cloud services, accounts, analytics, or support-file uploads.
